Questions

Good to know

Is RacquetTribe a court-booking app?

No. Keep booking courts wherever you do today. We help you find the people to play with, and make running the game easy once you're there.

What kind of stories will I see?

Tournaments and their results, big wins from players around you, new courts opening and court news — from your neighbourhood all the way to international.

How do badges work?

You earn them by playing, winning, keeping your streak going and hitting milestones. They show on your profile, so the more you play, the more the community gets to know you.

What's the difference between a public and a private game?

A public game shows up for players nearby so anyone can join. A private game is invite-only — perfect for your regulars or your tribe.

Which sports?

We're starting with pickleball, and bringing in padel and other racquet sports after that.

When does it launch?

The beta opens soon with a small group of players. Waitlist members get in first.
